Keep the Receipt

Never lose money on a purchase you meant to return.

Keep the Receipt helps you track what you bought, where you bought it, and when the return window closes — so you're not scrambling to find a receipt or guessing the deadline.

Features

  • Log items with store name, product, return-by date, and optional purchase date
  • See how many days you have left on active items
  • Return reminders around 9:00 AM so you can decide in time
  • Organize items as Active, Kept, or Returned
  • Mark items returned or kept from notifications
  • Export and import your entries as JSON across devices

Subscription

Keep the Receipt requires a subscription after a 14-day free trial. After the trial, the app is $1.99/month. Cancel anytime in the App Store or Google Play before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
